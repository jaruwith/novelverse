# NovelVerse Wireframe Scope

NovelVerse is a system-design prototype for Thai novels and comics under the J007lnwza brand, planned for `j007lnwza.com/novelverse`. It validates public discovery and reading, member publishing, and administration on desktop and mobile.

## Included

- Public discovery, search, category, story, reader, creator, following, history, and login experiences
- Member publishing dashboard, profile, works, chapters, comments, analytics, and settings
- Admin overview and user/content/taxonomy moderation
- Guest, Member, and Admin review states using a local role switcher
- Local Thai mock data and clickable, non-persistent controls
- Loading, empty, error, not-found, permission, archived, hidden, confirmation, and login-required states

## Excluded

Real authentication, databases, APIs, uploads, storage, payments, email, notifications, production security, analytics collection, and deployment.

## Validation goals

Review navigation, page hierarchy, role access, publishing and moderation flows, responsive behavior, content density, terminology, and system states before visual branding or engineering architecture is finalized.
